Assignment 01 - Top Five Ways to Waste Time

CMPA 3304 - Early Fall 2026

  1. Watch Family Guy Reaction Videos

    I enjoy watching people react to various Family Guy compilations on YouTube.

  2. Watch Tech Sports Videos

    I often search for Tech athletics content either from Tech or the media. It's a good way to waste some time.

  3. Watch Music Reaction Videos

    It's interesting seeing people react to a a band I'm familiar with, but they've never seen.

  4. Watch the News

    A good way to waste some time, but maybe learn something is to watch a few minutes of cable news. The stories repeat throughout the day, so it doesn't take long to know what's happening.

  5. Doing Nothing in Particular

    Just sitting down and watching some show or movie that you don't really have to pay much attention to is the easiest way to waste time. You can just sit back and relax, and not have to think about anything.
